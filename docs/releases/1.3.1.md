### Highlights

#### Fixed Version Mismatch With Vanilla Players

Players running session mods such as the Trainer can join vanilla players again, and vanilla players can join them. Content that can't switch off live, such as legacy imports, still keeps vanilla players out of public lobbies only.

#### Reworked the Bar and the Mods Tab

Moved the **Wiki** to an icon beside **Settings** and removed the **Mods** title. The **Profile** button shows the profile name beside a chip of the profile color. The mod panel opens with a selected mod and takes one third of the width.

### Additions

- Added a message on the first arrival at the ship that says whether the deployed mods affect who can join.
- Added a custom color beside the presets for folders and profiles.

### Changes

- Changed game files no longer block deploys. The **Game files changed** warning opens a dialog that lists the files and how to restore them.
- Removed the separate matchmaking pool for mods every player needs. Those mods now switch off live instead.
- Changed the update prompt to two buttons, **Later** and **Install**, with **Changelog** as a link beside them.
- Raised the contrast of buttons, choices and links on the light theme, and added a thin ring to every scope diamond there.
- Renamed the **FAQ** to **F.A.Q** and brought the answers in line with the current multiplayer rules, save backups and Obsidian Patches.
