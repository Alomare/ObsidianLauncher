### Highlights

#### Guardrails

Added **Guardrails**, a shield at the top of the window in place of the multiplayer switch. It holds three switches for each profile, which apply from the next launch and which no mod can get past: **Allow other players' session mods in my game**, **Keep achievements from unlocking**, and **Freeze my account's progression**. A frozen account keeps the XP, samples, unlocks, upgrades and stats it had at launch, whatever a mission, the Armory or a mod changes. The shield fills and counts the last two while they're on, and the **Profiles** dialog marks a profile that has them. An imported profile keeps them and says so. Mods can read them with `obsidian.guardrails()`.

#### Session Mods Leave Your Save Alone

Removed the holds that session mods could put on your save. Missions played with session mods now pay XP, samples, unlocks, stats and achievements as usual, unless you freeze your progression in **Guardrails**. Galactic War results and leaderboards are still never sent while session mods are deployed, and Proving Grounds stays closed. `obsidian.session.forfeit_progress`, `release_progress` and `hold_progress` still exist for older mods, and do nothing.

#### Mods Without a Scope Run as Client Mods

Added an **unspecified** scope, shown as a grey diamond, for mods that don't declare one and for older imported mods. They run as client mods, so they no longer keep you out of multiplayer. Older imports already in your library change over by themselves, and a mod marked `every_player` still counts as a session mod.

#### Obsidian API 1.7.1

Updated Obsidian API to 1.7.1. Mods can now share short texts between the players in a lobby, find a point of the level on the screen, place units that only one player sees, show a game screen with their own words, tune the stats and melee of one weapon without touching others of the same type, give drone pack drones without the backpack, and call in a stratagem for another player on the same PC. Steam achievements now unlock with the API on, and the game's `upgrade_all` console command is allowed.

#### Weapon Tuning in Obsidian Trainer

Added the fire rate, damage, magazine, reload, spread and armor piercing of each of your own weapons to the **Weapons** page of Obsidian Trainer, now at 1.3.1.

### Additions

- Added a **Guardrails** page to the Wiki, and translated every Obsidian API passage added since 1.0.3.

### Bug Fixes
