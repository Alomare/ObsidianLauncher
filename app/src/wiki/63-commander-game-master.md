## The war screen

Opening a war from the war list shows the war screen. The bar on top holds the war name with the season and state, the war clock, **Share**, **Settings**, the More menu and **Select**. Under it, the galaxy map draws the war map from the game, with each front in the color of the enemy, and the strip below the map shows how far each front has come. The Events panel on the map lists the running defenses and attacks with the time each has left.

The side panel has four tabs, also on the keys 1 to 4: **Command**, **Feed**, **Players** and **Statistics**. Escape clears the selection on the map.

## Clock and speed

War time runs while the war server runs and the clock isn't paused. The ring button beside the clock pauses and resumes it, and so does Space. While the clock is paused, event countdowns stop too. The speed buttons set how much war time passes each real second, from 0.5× to 10×. Enemy pressure, event durations and the war director all follow war time. No war time passes while Obsidian is closed on the host PC, and the war picks up where it stopped at the next start.

## Orders

Clicking a front on the strip, a region on the map or Super Earth in the middle opens the orders for it in the **Command** tab. Every order shows in the Feed, marked GM.

| Order | What it does |
|---|---|
| Enemy Push | The enemy takes one region back |
| Liberate | Helldivers take one region |
| Move Front Here | Makes the selected region the front line |
| Front Points | Sets the points of the front to any value, with the slider or the number |
| Start Defense | Starts a defense in the current region of the front, or in the selected region with **Start Defense Here**, with the duration and goal set above the button. One defense runs at a time |
| Start Attack | Starts the attack on the enemy home world, with the duration and goal set above the button |
| Introduce Front | Brings a front that isn't in the war yet into it, with the points it had |
| Remove Front | Takes a front out of the war. Running events on it end, players no longer see it, and the points wait for **Introduce Front** |
| Mark Defeated | Counts the enemy on this front as beaten. With every front defeated, the season is won |
| Reopen Front | Brings a defeated enemy back, holding the last two regions |

Super Earth takes one order: a defense against the front picked in the panel. Selecting a running event shows the progress, sets the event points, or ends the event at once with **End as Success** or **End as Failure**.

## Seasons

A season is won when every front is defeated and lost when Super Earth falls. Either way the war stops until **New Season**, and the game plays the recap and season-end screens when players next load in. **End Season** in the Command tab ends the running season with a win or a loss. **New Season** starts the next one from the start points: the statistics start over, while the players and the history stay.

## Feed, Players and Statistics

**Feed** lists everything that happened, newest first: missions, regions won and lost, events, new players and orders. **Players** lists everyone who has played the war, who is online now, when each was last seen and how many missions each played. **Statistics** totals the season (missions and success rate, planets, kills and accuracy, deaths and accidental kills) per front, and the Front Progress chart follows each front over the last day, the last week or the whole war.
