summary: The guides, how to find a topic, and the Wiki links across the app.
---

The Wiki holds the guides, in two subtabs: **Launcher** for mods, saves and playing online, and **Commander** for custom wars. The FAQ and Troubleshooting sit at the top of each list.

The search box over the list finds every guide that mentions a word. On a page, **Ctrl+F** finds a word on that page, and the outline at the right jumps to a heading. **Previous** and **Next** at the foot of a page follow the guides in order.

Across the app, a tooltip with a **WIKI** tag means a click opens the guide on that topic, such as the scope icons in the Mods tab and the **?** beside headings.
