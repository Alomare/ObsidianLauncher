## Modded missions don't count

Every player shares one Galactic War. A session mod that makes missions easier, or harder, would push the whole war in one direction for everyone else.

While any session content is deployed, the loader stops your game from reporting mission results and statistics to the shared war. Your own save still updates: you keep the XP, unlocks and medals you earn.

Client mods don't change this. With only client mods, your missions count as normal.

## Proving Grounds stays closed

Proving Grounds is ranked, with leaderboards and capes for the best times. While session content is deployed, Obsidian doesn't open it: picking it on the galaxy map shows a message instead, and a trial you picked before is dropped, so the ship doesn't start it either. The same goes for a player who lets other players' mods in, while someone in the lobby runs a different set.

> [!NOTE]
> The block applies as long as session content is deployed, even in a mission where your session mods are switched off. Purge or deploy without session mods to take part in the war again.
