## Modded missions don't count

Every player shares one Galactic War. A session mod that makes missions easier, or harder, would push the whole war in one direction for everyone else.

While any session content is deployed, the loader stops the game from reporting mission results and statistics to the shared war. The save still updates, so the XP, unlocks and medals earned stay. Client mods don't change this: with only client mods, missions count as normal.

A custom war from [Commander](app:commander) is the exception. Missions in a custom war report only to the war server of that war, so missions with session mods count there.

## Proving Grounds stays closed

Proving Grounds is ranked, with leaderboards and capes for the best times. While session content is deployed, Obsidian doesn't open it: picking it on the galaxy map shows a message instead, and a trial picked before is dropped, so the ship doesn't start it either. The same goes for a player who lets session mods from other players in, while someone in the lobby runs a different set.

> [!NOTE]
> The block applies as long as session content is deployed, even in a mission where the session mods are switched off. Purge, or deploy without session mods, to take part in the war again.

## Expansions

An expansion deployed changes nothing here until the content starts. While the content runs, Proving Grounds is closed, and the results of every mission the content runs in stay out of the war. Those results wait on the ship after the content ends, and are dropped when the next mission starts. That next mission, without the content, counts as normal.
