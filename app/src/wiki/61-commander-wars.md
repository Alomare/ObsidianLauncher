## Create War

**Create War** on the war list opens a form with a name, the model and a preset. A **Vanilla War** follows the campaign built into the game: three fronts of ten regions each, defenses, home world attacks and Super Earth under siege.

| Preset | For |
|---|---|
| Standard | The default numbers. Pushing a front home takes several evenings of play |
| Short Campaign | Fewer points per front and gentler enemy pressure, for a campaign that fits in a weekend |
| Hard | Stronger enemy pressure, frequent defenses and costly events |

**Advanced** under the presets lists every rule of the war, and **Settings** on the war screen changes the same rules while the war runs. [War rules](wiki:commander-rules) explains each one. A new war starts with the clock running.

## War info

**Edit War Info**, in the More menu of the war screen or **Edit** on a war card, sets the name, a description for the players who join and a logo. A square picture works best for the logo: PNG, JPEG or WebP, shrunk to 256 pixels. Without a logo the war shows the logo of the model.

## Export, Import and Delete

**Export War** in the More menu saves the whole war as one JSON file, history and statistics included. **Import War** on the war list adds a war from such a file, which also moves a war to another PC. The imported copy is a separate war: players who joined the old one join the new host again.

**Delete War** removes the war, the history and the statistics for good. Export first to keep a copy. The war save stays in [Saves](app:saves) until deleted there.
