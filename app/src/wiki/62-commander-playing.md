## Select a war

**Select** on a war card makes that war the one **Play** starts, and the card and the war screen then show **Selected**. The menu button beside **Play**, in the bar at the top of Obsidian, opens the same choice for every war at once: **Official Galactic War** first, then the wars this PC hosts and the wars it joined, each with the saves it can boot, and **Apply**. Picking **Official Galactic War** there goes back to the official war. Wars can only be switched while the game is closed.

## The war save

A war plays on a Sandbox save, picked in the menu beside **Play**: any Sandbox, or **New Sandbox Save**, which Obsidian makes for the war, with the tutorials already done, when the war is selected with no save picked. When the host switches on **Own Save Only** in **War Settings**, the war plays only on the save made for it.

The rank, XP and unlocks earned in a war stay on the Sandbox save it was played on, and the Official save doesn't change. The war save shows in [Saves](app:saves) like any other Sandbox.

A Sandbox needs the Obsidian loader in the game. With nothing deployed, **Play** asks to deploy first, and a game started without the loader, from Steam for example, plays the official war on the Official save.

## In the game

The galaxy map in the game shows the custom war: the fronts, the regions, the running events and the season. Missions work as usual. A completed planet moves the front, and missions on a defense or a home world attack fill that event.

Every war has a separate matchmaking pool. Players only find lobbies of the war they selected, so a squad selects the same war before meeting in the game.

## When the war server is out of reach

The game checks the war every 60 seconds. When no answer comes, because the host closed Obsidian or the network dropped, the game shows the war as offline and keeps trying. Missions finished while the war is offline don't count. The war clock of the host stops whenever the war server is closed, so no defense runs out while nobody can reach the war.
