The [Wars](app:commander) tab runs custom Galactic Wars with Obsidian Commander: wars with rules set by the host, played in the regular game.

The row at the top switches between the wars on this PC and wars joined over the local network. **Join War** finds a war on another PC, **Import** reads a war from a file and **New War** creates one. The package button at the right installs or updates Obsidian Commander.

A war card opens the map of the war. **Select** makes the war the one **Play** starts, with a save the war accepts.

The Commander subtab of this Wiki covers wars in full, starting with [Obsidian Commander](wiki:commander).
